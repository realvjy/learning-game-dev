# Contributing Guidelines

Thank you for your interest in contributing! All contributions are welcome.

## How to Contribute

1. **Fork the repository** to your own GitHub account.
2. **Clone the project** to your local machine.
3. **Create a new branch** for your feature or fix.
4. **Make your changes** (e.g., adding documentation or images).
5. **Commit your changes** with descriptive commit messages.
6. **Push the changes** to your fork.
7. **Open a Pull Request** against the main repository.

## Reporting Issues

If you find a typo, have a suggestion, or want to report an issue, please open an issue in the repository with a clear description.
